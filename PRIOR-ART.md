# Prior art and attribution

- Che, Z., Purushotham, S., Cho, K., Sontag, D., Liu, Y. (2018).
  Recurrent Neural Networks for Multivariate Time Series with Missing Values.
  *Scientific Reports* 8, 6085. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-24271-9
  GRU-D uses observation masks, time intervals and decay mechanisms. MIRA-CSI
  does not claim to invent the use of masks or gap information.
- Dempster, A., Schmidt, D. F., Webb, G. I. (2021).
  MiniRocket: A Very Fast (Almost) Deterministic Transform for Time Series
  Classification. *KDD*, 248–257. https://doi.org/10.1145/3447548.3467231
  Official basic multivariate reference:
  https://github.com/angus924/minirocket, commit
  `0b1c245d9c9dbc50886f28bc7b32d5d45b5663d6`.
- Alsaify, B. A., Almazari, M. M., Alazrai, R., Daoud, M. I. (2020).
  A dataset for Wi-Fi-based human activity recognition in line-of-sight and
  non-line-of-sight indoor environments. *Data in Brief* 33, 106534.
  https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2020.106534

This is a focused starting record, not an exhaustive novelty search. Evidence
of a statistically reliable benefit, reproducibility and a wider literature
review are required before claiming a new scientific advance.
