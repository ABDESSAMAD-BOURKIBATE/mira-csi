# Verified frozen outputs

These tables retain all reported conditions, including adverse results.
Five mask realizations are averaged inside each training seed; means and
sample SD then summarize five independently fitted seeds on the fixed data.
Accuracy/F1 values in CSV are fractions unless the column explicitly states
percent. Participant-bootstrap ranges are conditional on fixed rooms and
models, exploratory and uncorrected for model selection.

Full saved-output evidence and portable auditors accompany research release
v0.3.0. Consult the manuscript and [spectral model card](../../spectral/MODEL-CARD.md)
before interpreting differences as architectural or causal effects.
